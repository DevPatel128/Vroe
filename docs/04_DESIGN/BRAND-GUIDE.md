# Vroe Labs Brand Guide

**Status:** Approved · **Last updated:** 2026-09-01 · **Owner:** Vroe Labs · **Version:** 1.0

## Brand direction

Warm, editorial, useful, and quietly optimistic. Vroe Labs should feel like a small studio with strong taste: thoughtful products, clear language, and visual confidence without unnecessary polish.

## Typography

- Display: `Instrument Serif`, with `Georgia` as the fallback. Use for headlines, product names, and expressive statements.
- Interface: `DM Sans`, with `sans-serif` as the fallback. Use for navigation, labels, buttons, body copy, and product UI.
- Display style: regular weight, tight tracking, compact line-height, and generous surrounding space.
- Body style: 13-15px on desktop, 13px on mobile, with a 1.55-1.6 line-height.
- Eyebrows: uppercase, 10px, 700 weight, and 0.16em letter spacing.

## Layout and shape

- Content width: 1200px for primary sections, 1360px for the hero and header.
- Desktop page gutters: 80px; mobile page gutters: 20px.
- Primary card radius: 20px; controls: 10-12px; pills: 999px.
- Use asymmetry, oversized serif headlines, and one strong visual object per section.
- Keep backgrounds atmospheric with photography, soft texture, or blocks of colour rather than flat white alone.

## Voice

- Say what the product makes easier.
- Prefer specific, human language over feature lists.
- Leave room for curiosity, but never imply a product is live when it is not.
- Avoid hype, invented metrics, and claims that cannot be demonstrated.
