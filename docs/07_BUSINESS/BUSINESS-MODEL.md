# Business model

**Status:** Approved · **Last updated:** 2026-09-20 · **Owner:** Vroe Labs · **Version:** 1.0

**No revenue model is decided or implemented.** The site sells nothing: it takes no
payments, has no pricing and shows no offers, and its structured data carries none
([ADR-006](../08_DECISIONS/PRODUCT/ADR-006-trove-is-not-presented-as-live.md)). What
Trove or Vero will charge, or whether they will, is not decided in this repository, so
none is stated: it would be a claim about a product that has not shipped.

## What is real

| | State |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue | None. There is no payment path, and rule 1 forbids implying one |
| Infrastructure cost | $0 a month: Cloudflare's free tier only ([ADR-018](../08_DECISIONS/ENGINEERING/ADR-018-infrastructure-cost-review-cloudflare-free-tier-only.md), [COST.md](../05_ENGINEERING/COST/COST.md)) |
| Domain | An annual registration paid to the registrar, Cloudflare. The amount is not recorded here |
| Maintainer's time | Not recorded |
| Pricing for Trove or Vero | Not decided here |

## Open decisions

For the maintainer, and not for this document to settle: whether and how each product
is monetised, what "early access" entitles someone to (today it entitles them to an
email and nothing else, and the terms say so), and whether any of this belongs in the
public repository or with the company. When one is decided, it goes here with its
source and date, and as an ADR.
