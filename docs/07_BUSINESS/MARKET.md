**No market claim is made.** The site presents evidence about a *problem*, sourced
and dated, in [RESEARCH.md](../03_RESEARCH/RESEARCH.md). That is not a market size,
and it is not presented as one. The framework requires a market claim to carry its
definition, methodology, source and date; none exists here, so none is stated.
