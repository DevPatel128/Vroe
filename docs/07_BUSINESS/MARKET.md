# Market

**Status:** Approved · **Last updated:** 2026-09-20 · **Owner:** Vroe Labs · **Version:** 1.0

**No market claim is made.** The framework requires a market claim to carry its
definition, methodology, source and date, and none exists here.

## What is real

The site presents evidence about a *problem*, not a market. `/trove` shows sourced,
dated figures for the cost of managing personal finances in two countries, India and
the United States, all collected and published in 2024 or later
([RESEARCH.md](../03_RESEARCH/RESEARCH.md), [SOURCES.md](../03_RESEARCH/SOURCES.md)).
Every figure is recomputed from its source data by `tests/evidence.test.mjs`. Eight
other countries were researched and removed because none had a recent verified
measure ([ADR-017](../08_DECISIONS/PRODUCT/ADR-017-only-data-collected-and-published-in-2024-or-later.md)).

That is a description of a problem people have. It is not a market size, a share of
one, or a forecast, and nothing here converts it into one.

## Not stated

Market size, growth, share, competitors and segments. Vero has no research base at
all yet. When a market claim is made it goes here with its definition, method, source
and date, and the evidence layer's rules apply to it.
