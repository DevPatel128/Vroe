# Thesis

**Status:** Approved · **Last updated:** 2026-09-20 · **Owner:** Vroe Labs · **Version:** 1.0

Why the products this site describes should exist, written only as far as the
evidence and the site's own honesty rules allow. Where a section would need a
claim the site cannot support, it says so instead of making one.

## Core belief

Two beliefs are already published on the site, in the products' own words
(`src/content/products.js`):

- **Trove:** "Most people do not have a money problem so much as a
  money-visibility problem."
- **Vero:** work history is hard to prove, and proof should belong to the person
  who earned it.

## Problem

- **Trove.** The picture of a person's money is scattered across accounts, cards
  and subscriptions, and comes together only in a spreadsheet somebody maintains
  by hand. The measured costs of managing personal finances, India first, are in
  [RESEARCH.md](../03_RESEARCH/RESEARCH.md). They describe the problem, never an
  effect Trove has had.
- **Vero.** A CV is a claim, a portfolio can be copied, and a rating says more
  about one transaction than about the person. Vero is an exploration; there is no
  research base for it yet.

## Insight

What others are missing is stated in each product's principles in `products.js`
(data stays yours, manual control is a feature, history should stay true; proof
should belong to whoever earned it). These are beliefs, not findings, and are not
presented as evidence.

## Why now

**Not written.** No verified account of what changed exists in this repository,
and inventing one would break the rule against fabricating.

## Product answer

Described as intent, never as shipped features, in [CONTENT.md](../04_DESIGN/CONTENT.md).
Neither product has shipped, so no claim about how well either solves the problem
is made anywhere.

## Differentiation, long-term direction, defensibility

**Not written.** Each would assert something about a product that has not shipped,
which [PRINCIPLES.md](../01_PRINCIPLES/PRINCIPLES.md) rule 1 forbids on the public
site and which the framework's evidence rule forbids without a source.

## Evidence

Every material claim links to [RESEARCH.md](../03_RESEARCH/RESEARCH.md) or a source
in [SOURCES.md](../03_RESEARCH/SOURCES.md). Nothing here stands without one.

## Falsifiers

**Not yet defined.** The framework asks what evidence would prove the thesis wrong.
That needs the maintainer's judgement about what would count, and is left open
rather than guessed.

## Decision

`ACTIVE`, in the narrow sense that the site presents these beliefs today.
Reason: the maintainer's stated direction.

Approved by Dev, 2026-09-20, as it stands: partly written on purpose. The four
sections marked *Not written* stay open until there is something true to put in them
(a verified account of why now; a shipped product to differentiate; the maintainer's
judgement of what would count as proof the thesis is wrong).
