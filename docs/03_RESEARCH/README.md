# 03 Research

**Status:** Review · **Last updated:** 2026-09-19 · **Owner:** Vroe Labs · **Version:** 1.0

- **Purpose:** the evidence behind every external claim on the site.
- **Belongs here:** current conclusions and figures, the source index, the raw and
  derived data, and superseded research.
- **Does not belong here:** product definition ([02_PRODUCT](../02_PRODUCT/README.md)),
  decisions taken because of the research ([08_DECISIONS](../08_DECISIONS/DECISIONS.md)).
- **Important files:** [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md) (conclusions, method and rules),
  [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) (every source, with URL and checksum),
  [impact-research/](impact-research/README.md) (raw files, scripts, derived data),
  [ARCHIVE/](ARCHIVE/README.md) (superseded and unused).
- **Source of truth:** RESEARCH.md for what we know; SOURCES.md for where it came
  from. Research distinguishes fact, evidence-backed inference, assumption,
  hypothesis, projection and unknown, and never presents one as another.
