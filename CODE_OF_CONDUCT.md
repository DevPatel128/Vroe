# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a respectful, harassment-free space for everyone who
takes part, whatever their background or experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Keep feedback specific and about the work, not the person.
- Accept that people disagree, and disagree constructively.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that
break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.

## Reporting

Report a problem to vroelabs@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
